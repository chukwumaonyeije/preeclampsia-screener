Aspirin Prophylaxis Eligibility Assessment

⚠️ INFORMATIONAL USE ONLY: This tool is designed for educational purposes and clinical decision support. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. All therapeutic decisions, including aspirin prophylaxis, must be discussed with and prescribed by a qualified healthcare provider (obstetrician, maternal-fetal medicine specialist, or other appropriate clinician).

Clinical Guidance: Low-dose aspirin (81mg daily) reduces preeclampsia risk when started between 12-28 weeks of gestation (optimally before 16 weeks) and continued until delivery.

Recommendation Criteria: Aspirin is indicated if patient has ≥1 high-risk factor OR ≥2 moderate-risk factors.

  • Maternal-fetal medicine (MFM) specialist
  • Primary care physician or other qualified healthcare professional

Always consider individual patient circumstances, contraindications to aspirin therapy (including bleeding disorders, aspirin allergy, and other medical conditions), and patient preferences before initiating any treatment.

If you are pregnant or planning pregnancy, consult your healthcare provider before starting any medication, including over-the-counter aspirin.
